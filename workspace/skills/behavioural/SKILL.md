# Behavioural Interview Skill

Load this skill only for behavioural interview rounds.

Evaluate behavioural answers using the STAR structure:

- Situation: Did the candidate explain the context?
- Task: Did they clarify their responsibility or goal?
- Action: Did they describe their own actions specifically?
- Result: Did they state an outcome, ideally measurable?
- Reflection: Did they explain what they learned when relevant?

Do not require a perfect corporate story. Reward clarity, ownership, specificity,
honesty, and reflection. Ask one behavioural question at a time. Do not invent
candidate experiences or suggest that a fabricated story is acceptable.
